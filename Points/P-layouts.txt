						<< Floats >> 
when an element is float it is taken aout from the page like absolute positioning and act as it do not exist in that place therefore its "parrent" div height become zero or its fill area become zero
all other element float(wrape) around it but that  floated element do no overlap
floated elemet act as inline block as margins and padding is applied
1: out of flow
2: other elements wrape around it
3: clear the floats of other elements
4:pseodu element are inline and float can be cleared only by block elements
flaots are cleared in order to make other elements on its actual place
 summary: upon floating the element(childs) the parrent fill area will zero if parrent does not exist simply then no fill area is there and all other elemets will be float around it.
and it will not overlap the other elements(characteristic of floats)
